# Core Game Rules

## Game and movement

- Build a 2D side-scrolling survival RPG in Unity.
- The player can face and move both left and right.
- Enemies can face and move both left and right, depending on their target, patrol direction, or combat state.

## Stage progression

- Each stage must be cleared before progression.
- A stage portal remains inactive until all required enemies and objectives are cleared.
- Once those requirements are met, the stage portal activates and allows progression.

## Combat

- Combat should reward creative use of hazards and environmental interactions.

## Visual direction

- Vibrant modern pixel art.
- High-fantasy ruins.
- Blues, greens, and stone.
